How it works / tips
  • PDF: must be a vector PDF exported from CAD (scanned images are not supported). Paths are grouped by PDF layer (if present) or by line style. Filled shapes are off by default.
  • DXF: read directly (lines, arcs, circles, ellipses, polylines, splines, blocks).
  • DWG: read in the browser with LibreDWG (WASM, ~8 MB, downloaded on first use). If a DWG fails, convert it to DXF with the free ODA File Converter or “Save As DXF” in AutoCAD and load the DXF.
  • Extrude: closed profiles are extruded to the given thickness. Use “Select region” to pick one part on a sheet with a border / title block.
  • Revolve: draw or select a half-profile with its centre line; “Detect centre line” finds the longest dashed axis-parallel line. Edges (chamfer / fillet) round the profile corners before spinning.
  • 3 views: front, top and side silhouettes are extruded and intersected (visual hull). Switch on “Read dashed lines” to carve blind holes and pockets whose depth is shown by hidden lines in another view (experimental, check the result).
  • Edges: chamfer / fillet apply to sharp outline corners and, when extruding, to the top and bottom edges. Keep the size below half of the thinnest wall.
  • STEP: exports a solid made of planar faces (curved surfaces are faceted, like STL but as a real B-rep). Good for measuring and as a reference in CAD / robot offline programming.
  • Axes: X = width (front view horizontal), Y = depth, Z = height. STL / OBJ are exported in mm (Z up); GLB in metres (Y up, glTF standard).
  • 2D view: drag = pan, wheel = zoom, double-click = fit.
